MULTI-AGENT ORCHESTRATION — ELITE PERFORMANCE & PROMOTION REVIEW

Act as the Principal Orchestrator, Executive Performance Strategist, and final Promotion Committee Evaluator.

Do not perform the entire analysis as a single agent. Use Claude Code’s available subagent capabilities to delegate independent, specialized investigations to multiple agents. Each agent must analyze the existing performance-review document and relevant evidence from its own perspective, then return structured findings to the main agent.

1. Spawn specialist subagents

Use these analytical personas as separate agents:

1. Peter Drucker Agent — Organizational Impact: Identify business outcomes, organizational effectiveness, reusable capabilities, and impact beyond assigned responsibilities.
2. Andy Grove Agent — Execution & Results: Analyze delivery, measurable productivity, ownership, operational performance, and execution excellence.
3. W. Edwards Deming Agent — Quality & Reliability: Analyze engineering quality, automated testing, production stability, root-cause resolution, risk controls, and continuous improvement.
4. Jeff Bezos Agent — Customer & Business Value: Identify customer impact, ownership, long-term value, operational savings, and high-leverage initiatives.
5. Kim Scott Agent — Leadership & Values: Analyze collaboration, integrity, influence, knowledge sharing, developing others, and demonstrated leadership behaviours.
6. Technical Evidence Auditor: Independently validate metrics, calculations, dates, stakeholder quotations, adoption claims, production outcomes, and consistency across the review and available source evidence.
7. Promotion Committee Agent: Evaluate the evidence against official WHAT/HOW rating definitions and next-level engineering competencies. Identify the strongest promotion arguments, weaknesses, and likely committee objections.

If subagent concurrency is supported, run independent analyses in parallel. Give each agent a clearly scoped task and access only to the relevant authorized evidence.

2. Require structured findings from every agent

Each agent must return:

* The five strongest findings within its scope.
* Supporting evidence and source references.
* Business or organizational significance.
* Relevant WHAT, HOW, and promotion competencies.
* Verified metrics versus estimates or unverified claims.
* Missing evidence, contradictions, duplication, and risks.
* Specific recommendations for strengthening the review.

Agents must not invent facts, modify the original review, or independently produce competing final versions.

3. Main-agent synthesis and challenge

After collecting the reports, the main agent must:

1. Cross-check findings across agents and resolve contradictions.
2. Eliminate duplicated achievements and unsupported claims.
3. Prioritize evidence by demonstrated impact, organizational reach, leadership, and next-level relevance.
4. Distinguish individual contributions from team outcomes.
5. Select the strongest evidence for outstanding WHAT, outstanding HOW, and promotion readiness.
6. Challenge its own conclusions as a skeptical senior promotion committee.
7. Ask for confirmation wherever critical facts or evidence remain unresolved.

Do not mechanically combine every agent’s recommendations. Exercise independent judgment and retain only the strongest, relevant, non-duplicative findings.

4. Final deliverable ownership

The MAIN AGENT alone owns the final deliverables:

* One cohesive, executive-ready performance review.
* Separate, compelling WHAT and HOW narratives.
* A defensible promotion-readiness case mapped to official criteria.
* An evidence matrix supporting the major claims.
* A concise manager-discussion brief.
* A prioritized list of unresolved evidence gaps.

The final review must read as one coherent narrative, not as a collection of AI-generated reports.

5. Execution and safety rules

First inspect the existing document, available source evidence, official competency criteria, and the available subagent tools. Delegate only work that can be performed with the actual capabilities available; do not pretend agents ran if they did not.

Preserve the original file. Write the consolidated result to a separate proposed file. Do not submit the review or communicate with anyone on my behalf.

Optimize for the strongest evidence-based case for outstanding WHAT, outstanding HOW, and promotion to the next engineering level. Accuracy, independent verification, and demonstrated impact take precedence over persuasive wording.
